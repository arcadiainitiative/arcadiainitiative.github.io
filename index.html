Founded June 9, 2025

What We Are

We’re a crew of college students with fire in our veins. You’ve got ideas that keep you up at night. You’re ready to team up and build something bigger than any one of us.

Our Mission

We’re here to bring ambitious students like you together to create something epic as a team. Your idea might spark it—research that breaks new ground, a startup that flips the game, or a solution to a real problem. We’ll bounce ideas, refine them, and build one project together. Our connections open doors. If our shared idea shines, we’ve got people who might fund it big.

Who We Seek

We’re after students who live with fire. You’ve proven you can outwork everyone, whether in classes, projects, or side gigs. Your major doesn’t matter. Your drive to collaborate and create something lasting does.

Why Join

You’ll join a tight group of students who match your energy. Together, we’ll tap into our network of insiders to push our project forward. If what we build has that spark, we know folks who might pour serious cash into it. This is your chance to create something that changes the game.

Application Process

We’re keeping it small, just five of us. Ready to join the crew? Hit us up at contact us. Share your story, your ideas (or just your drive), and why you’re ready to build with us.
